Acceptable Use Policy

This Acceptable Use Policy (“AUP”) describes conduct that is prohibited when using Matrix. It is part of, and incorporated into, the Terms of Service. Violating this AUP is a breach of the Terms.

Effective: June 7, 2026 Last updated: June 7, 2026 Version: 1.0

01 Overview

You are responsible for everything you do through Matrix, including all Intents you create and all transactions you authorize. You agree to use the Service lawfully and not to use it — or enable any Agent to use it — for any prohibited purpose described below. This list is illustrative, not exhaustive.

02 Unlawful & Illicit-Finance Use

  • Any activity that violates applicable law or regulation;
  • Money laundering, terrorist financing, or financing of proliferation;
  • Concealing or disguising the origin, source, or ownership of assets;
  • Processing proceeds of crime, fraud, theft, ransomware, or other illicit activity;
  • Tax evasion or facilitating it;
  • Trafficking in unlawful goods or services, including illegal drugs, weapons, or stolen data.

03 Sanctions & Restricted Persons

  • Using the Service if you are a Restricted Person or located in a comprehensively sanctioned jurisdiction;
  • Transacting, directly or indirectly, with sanctioned persons, entities, or addresses;
  • Evading, or attempting to evade, sanctions or export-control laws, including by obscuring your location or identity.

04 Market & Trading Abuse

  • Market manipulation, including spoofing, layering, wash trading, pump-and-dump, or front-running;
  • Trading on the basis of material non-public information where unlawful;
  • Manipulating or attempting to manipulate oracles, price feeds, order books, batch auctions, or settlement mechanisms;
  • Exploiting bugs, mispricings, or vulnerabilities to extract value to which you are not entitled;
  • Operating fraudulent token launches, deceptive schemes, or Ponzi/pyramid structures.

05 Attacks on the Service or Protocol

  • Gaining or attempting unauthorized access to the Service, the Paxeer Network, accounts, or systems;
  • Introducing malware, exploits, or malicious code;
  • Denial-of-service attacks, spam, flooding, or otherwise disrupting the Service or network;
  • Probing, scanning, or testing vulnerabilities without authorization (other than under a published responsible-disclosure or bug-bounty program);
  • Interfering with validators, consensus, mempool, or network operations.

06 Misuse of AI Agents

  • Prompt injection, jailbreaking, or manipulating Agents to bypass safety, security, or compliance controls;
  • Using Agents to plan, automate, or carry out any activity prohibited by this AUP;
  • Extracting, copying, or reverse engineering models, prompts, or weights except as permitted by law;
  • Using Agents to generate unlawful, fraudulent, harassing, or deceptive content, or to impersonate others;
  • Delegating authority to an Agent for any purpose prohibited here, or in a manner you cannot responsibly monitor and revoke;
  • Using the Service to train competing models without authorization.

07 Harmful & Infringing Content

  • Infringing the intellectual-property, privacy, or other rights of others;
  • Content that is harassing, hateful, threatening, defamatory, or that exploits or endangers minors;
  • Distributing others’ personal or confidential data without authorization;
  • Misrepresenting your identity, affiliation, or the origin of content.

08 System Integrity

  • Scraping, harvesting, or bulk-extracting data except via permitted interfaces;
  • Circumventing rate limits, access controls, geofencing, or usage restrictions;
  • Sharing, reselling, or providing unauthorized access to the Service;
  • Using bots or automation in violation of these rules or to gain an unfair advantage.

09 Enforcement

We may investigate suspected violations and take any action we consider appropriate, including warning you, removing content, restricting features, suspending or terminating access, applying geofencing, preserving and disclosing information to authorities as permitted or required by law, and pursuing legal remedies. Because the Service is non-custodial and the Protocol is decentralized, our ability to act may be technically limited, and we cannot reverse on-chain transactions.

10 Reporting Abuse

To report a violation of this AUP, contact abuse@paxeer.network. To report a security vulnerability, contact security@paxeer.network.
