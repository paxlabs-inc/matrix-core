Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Centra AI uses cookies and similar technologies, and how you can control them. It supplements the Privacy Policy.

Effective: June 7, 2026 Last updated: June 7, 2026 Version: 1.0

01 What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website or use an application. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, pixels, and software development kits (SDKs). We use these to operate the Service, remember your preferences, keep you signed in, and understand how the Service is used.

02 Types We Use

  • Strictly necessary — required for the Service to function, including security, session management, and connecting your Wallet. These cannot be switched off.
  • Functional — remember your preferences, such as language and interface settings.
  • Analytics / performance — help us understand usage so we can improve the Service. These are non-essential and set only with your consent where required.

03 Cookies We Set

Name (example) Type Purpose Duration
mx_session Strictly necessary Maintains your authenticated session Session
mx_csrf Strictly necessary Protects against cross-site request forgery Session
mx_prefs Functional Stores interface preferences (e.g., language, theme) Up to 12 months
mx_consent Strictly necessary Records your cookie consent choices Up to 12 months
_analytics_id Analytics Aggregated usage measurement Up to 13 months

Cookie names and durations above are representative and should be replaced with the actual values your deployment uses.

04 Local Storage & Wallet Connections

Centra AI may use browser local storage to keep interface state and to manage your Wallet connection. Connecting a Wallet may also involve technologies provided by your Wallet software, which is governed by that provider’s own policies. We never store your private keys.

05 Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies or similar technologies may be set by third parties we use for analytics, security, or infrastructure. Those parties process data under their own policies. We do not use cookies to sell your personal information or for cross-context behavioral advertising.

06 Managing Cookies

Where required, we ask for your consent to non-essential cookies through a cookie banner or settings control, and you can change your choices at any time. You can also control cookies through your browser settings — including blocking or deleting them — though blocking strictly necessary cookies may break parts of the Service. Most browsers also support a “Do Not Track” or Global Privacy Control signal; we honor these where required by law.

07 Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Material changes will be indicated by updating the “Last updated” date.
